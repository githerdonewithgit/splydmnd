Allen McLean

Allen McLean isn’t your typical DJ duo. Yeah, we know you’ve heard that before. This time it’s true! These two didn’t meet in high school and become BFFs. They didn’t find each other on Myspace, make music over the internet and become famous. They definitely didn’t meet at Electric Daisy Carnival (they hate the smell of daisies).

These two met at an AA meeting in another dimension known as Sector X. Don’t believe it? Read the warning posters left around NYC and their hospitality rider. It reads like a Lindsay Lohan unpaid club bill covered in unicorn blood. After liberating a small village in South America from the cruel hands of DJ Idontgiveafuckwhatiplay, DJs All-En1 (Jamaica) and DJ Clean (Trinidad) are either the DJs every party wants or every party fears. The energy is real with these two! It’s so real, they have to wear masks so they don’t face prosecution from the last kid that overdosed on the fear of God and good ass music!

Drawing from their individual Caribbean roots, love for all genres of music and a background of PTWMD (Post Traumatic Wack Music Disorder), Allen McLean is pushing sound barriers with what they like to call “CDM”: Caribbean Dance Music. While Caribbean music has influenced music culture, this duo is infusing their roots with all music to create an amazing party experience. Think of them as a post-apocalyptic hitmen DJ duo that’s here to save you from wack parties and your mom’s casserole.
